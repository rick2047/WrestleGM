# Product Overview

WrestleGM is a single-player, text-based wrestling booking simulation game. It puts the player in the role of a wrestling promoter who must manage their roster, book shows, and grow their promotion.

## Vision

The vision for WrestleGM is to create a deep and engaging wrestling simulation experience that is easy to learn but difficult to master. The game should appeal to both hardcore wrestling fans and casual players who enjoy management simulation games.

## Core Features

### Roster Management

-   **Wrestlers:** The game features a roster of fictional wrestlers, each with their own unique stats and attributes (Popularity, Stamina, Mic Skill, Alignment).
-   **Contracts & Cost:** Players must manage their budget, as each wrestler has a booking price that scales with their popularity.

### Show Booking

-   **Matches & Promos:** Players can book a variety of match types and promos to create entertaining shows.
-   **Card Structure:** Each show consists of a set number of slots that can be filled with either a match or a promo.

### Simulation

-   **Deterministic Engine:** The game uses a deterministic simulation engine to decide the outcome of matches and the quality of shows. This ensures that the game is fair and that the same inputs will always produce the same outputs.
-   **Ratings:** Matches, promos, and entire shows are rated based on a variety of factors, including wrestler popularity, match type, and rivalries.

### Economy & Progression

-   **Finances:** Players start with a set amount of money and must manage their finances to avoid bankruptcy. Show costs (wrestler bookings, match type costs) are deducted from earnings (gate income, merchandise).
-   **Rivalries:** Booking wrestlers against each other repeatedly can create rivalries, which can lead to higher-rated matches and increased fan interest.
-   **Stamina & Recovery:** Wrestlers expend stamina in matches and recover it over time. Players must manage their roster to avoid overworking their talent.

### Persistence

-   **Save/Load:** Players can save their progress in one of multiple save slots and continue their game later.
